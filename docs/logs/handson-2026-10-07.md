## Anomalies

1. **Story request got no reply for about 57 s.** `>> Tell me a story` (serial 280, 10:40:35); server logs `大模型收到用户消息` (161) and then no LLM completion or TTS until after the device abort at 10:41:32 (server 176). On the device `mode=thinking` (283) turns into `mode=speaking` 20.0 s later (289) with silence. The next utterance (`Jarvis, what time is it?`, 300) got "It's ten forty-one. Here's a story. Once upon a time" (305). Cause not visible in the logs.
2. **Likely self-echo barge-ins.** `>> It's 10:41` (309), `>> Here's the story` (318), `>> a whole happy shower` (347), `>> The children dance` (355) match the device's own TTS text ("It's ten forty-one. Here's a story...") rather than Jay's script. Each triggered a server `Abort message received` (server 191, 200, 240, 248), cut the story and produced `<< Yep` / `<< Yes` / `<< Yes` replies. Unconfirmed: no mic recording; Jay may have been echoing the story.
3. **Wake word audio is transcribed as garbage on every wake** (2/2): `Chavez` (246) and `charis` (406), so the first LLM turn is wasted (`Chavez?`, `I'm here...`). This is the same defect that makes item 7 fail.
4. **Session drop.** `E EspTcp: TCP receive failed: -1` at 162.3 s (serial 370), 4 s after `speaking -> listening` (369), 8.7 s after the last TTS; server `客户端断开连接` 10:42:35 (264). The device went to idle (375) and woke normally 6.9 s later (381). The last prompt was an unanswered follow-up ("Want to hear what Pip did the next day?").
5. **Second session ended without user speech:** server `结束对话，无需发送结束提示语` 10:43:25 (337), serial `Websocket disconnected` 211.8 s (444), 13 s after the bedside confirmation finished. Normal.
6. **Gesture count:** 21 `pick_up` vs 10 intended; no false `face_down`, `face_up` or `double_tap` (0 each); 0 gestures during the 261 s idle windows other than the two touch-adjacent pick-ups at 254 s and 275 s. First pick-up (158) and first panel swipe (159) fall 3.6-4.2 s after the USB reset, i.e. while the device was being handled.
7. **One `IMU read failed (1)` at 5.1 s** (serial 210); none after.
8. **Warnings seen:** `Server sample rate 16000 does not match device output sample rate 24000` (235, 395), once per session; `MCP接入点工具列表响应结果为空或格式错误` on every server connection. Both appear in every prior capture.
