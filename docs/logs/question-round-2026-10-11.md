## Defect 1: a WHEN_IDLE re-delivery of an open question never opens its card

Gateway behaviour is as specified: a question goes out SILENT with the first item, and as WHEN_IDLE with `speaks:true` after the 20 s voice window (service log L8, L10; L104, L108). The device applies the WHEN_IDLE item (`result=applied tier=when_idle speaks=1`, L1087), speaks "Which room?" (L1085-1090) and returns to idle with the card unopened. `FaceDisplay::ApplyArrivals` and `pending_open_` are fed only by `AgentUi::SetTasks` arrivals, which are produced for a new state or a new card id (`changed_state || new_card`, `agent_ui.h` ~L368-372); the same task, state and card with a higher tier is no arrival, no `agent ui: task=` line is logged and nothing queues the card for `WantAutoOpen`. Reproduced in 4 questions (run 1 Q, S, T; run 2 Q, S, T: all opened only through a tap on the complication). A tap earlier than the speech (10 s after the question, run 1 S L3430-3436) did not open the card either; the tap at ≥ 30 s did.

Effect: the question card shows only after a manual tap on the face complication, never by itself; the spoken "Which room?" has no card to answer on. Suggested fix: treat a tier upgrade (silent → when_idle/interrupt) of an open-card item as an arrival for `pending_open_` (without a second earcon or flash).
