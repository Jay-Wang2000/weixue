## Regressions in the merged voice path

**R1. Streaming STT: an empty stream result is accepted as silence** (`feat-stt-streaming` 2fb0874, decision 42). `server/overlay/core/providers/asr/google_stt_stream.py:286-304` (`_collect`) returns `problem=None` when a finished stream produced no final. `speech_to_text` (:257-270) then returns `""`: no fallback, no repeat prompt, no turn. Every `latest_short` miss on a VAD-voiced segment of at least `MIN_UTTERANCE_BYTES` therefore silently drops the user's request (40/51 here).
- Fix: add a problem `no_final` (streams finished, no non-empty final, segment over `MIN_UTTERANCE_BYTES`) and route it to the existing `fallback_model` batch on `artifacts.pcm_bytes`. Keep it out of `STREAM_PROBLEMS`: if chirp_3 is also empty, there is no turn and no repeat prompt. Cost: about +1.4-1.6 s on those turns (V:442, V:698). Changing when chirp_3 is called is a Google STT usage decision, so Jay must approve it before implementing.
- Instrument at the same time: per stream, frames/bytes queued at open (the burst size), frames sent before `half_close`, response count, whether the service ended the stream before half-close, and `_drop_streams` calls. Probe: on the scratch stack with `delete_audio: false`, capture wake segments and replay identical PCM through latest_short streaming (burst vs real-time pacing) and chirp_3 batch. That settles "model on this audio" versus "how the pre-roll is fed".

**R2. A wake-word-only segment becomes a turn whenever STT misspells "Jarvis".** `server/overlay/weixue_patches.py:178-196` drops only an exact `wakeup_words` match. The pre-roll-only segment (no live voice after `VAD_FLUSH_S`, which is exactly when the cue plays; `hold_wake_utterance` :216-243, `acknowledge_wake` :365-398) is still sent to STT. Here that gave 4 junk turns (`charters`, `Chavez`, `下载！`, `China。`); on 10-07 under chirp_3 it was 1 of 20. The handson run (10-07) shows the same with a human voice (`Chavez`, `charis`).
- Fix: when a segment ends after a wake with `conn.weixue_live_voice` still false, drop it before STT. It holds only the wake word the device already reported. Do this in `google_stt_stream._finish` (:220), or in the `note_speech_end` wrapper (:657) for both providers. Land this before or with R1's fallback; otherwise chirp_3 turns these segments into more `charters` turns.

**R3. English mislabelled as Mandarin at 0.910 bypasses the zh fallback.** `google_stt_stream.py:23-25, 259`: the threshold is 0.9, and latest_short reports 0.910 on Mandarin finals, so `China。` (V:196) and `下载！` (V:901) were taken as-is. These are the wake-only segments R2 removes, so R3 is low priority after R2. Options for Jay: leave it after R2, or fall back on every cmn-labelled final (+latency on all Mandarin turns).

Not regressions: the wake cue player and C3 fence, the echo guard, F03, Google TTS first-clause streaming, and the firmware double-tap abort.
